# Xacereum Brand Assets

Welcome to the official repository for Xacereum's brand assets. This repository is intended for cryptocurrency exchanges, partners, and media outlets that require official Xacereum branding materials.

## Assets Included

- **xacereum-logo.png**: The official high-resolution Xacereum logo with a transparent background. Suitable for light and dark themes.

## Brand Guidelines

When using our logo, please adhere to the following guidelines:
1. **Clear Space**: Always maintain a clear space around the logo to ensure visibility and impact.
2. **Proportions**: Do not stretch, compress, or alter the proportions of the logo.
3. **Colors**: Do not change the colors of the logo.
4. **Backgrounds**: Use the provided transparent PNG on backgrounds that provide sufficient contrast.

## Usage

These assets are provided for the sole purpose of promoting Xacereum and representing it accurately on trading platforms and media.

If you require any other formats (e.g., SVG, EPS) or have specific branding inquiries, please reach out to our team at [support@xacereum.com](mailto:support@xacereum.com).
